## English

ScreenShift is **free for personal use only**, under the [ScreenShift Personal Use License](LICENSE): an individual, on their own computer, for their own private purposes, for example making their own damaged laptop screen usable at home. No payment, no registration.

**Every other use needs a paid commercial license.** The license text is the only authority, this page is a plain-language guide, not legal advice.

### When you need a commercial license

Any use that is not personal. Typical cases:

- companies and other for-profit organizations, and freelancers or sole proprietors working for clients
- schools, universities, charities, non-profits, research and government organizations
- computers owned or managed by an employer or an organization, including use for work
- a service of any kind, for example a repair shop applying it to customers' laptops, with or without a fee
- bundling, reselling or redistributing it, or building a product or service on its source code

Not sure whether your case counts? Ask first, the question is free.

### What you get

- the right to use, and where agreed to distribute, ScreenShift for your purpose
- written confirmation of the license for your records

Price and terms depend on how you use it (number of computers, distribution, support) and are agreed in writing. There is no public price list yet.

### How to get one

Write to **bluremind@gmail.com** with the subject `ScreenShift commercial license` and tell me:

1. who you are (organization or person) and where you are based
2. how you plan to use ScreenShift
3. how many computers or users

You will get an answer with a proposal.
